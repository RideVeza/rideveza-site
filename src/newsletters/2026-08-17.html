Week of August 17, 2026

Last week at Veza

Veza Web now tracks the days your schools are closed, splitting a run has been expanded, and the late details recorded on runs have become a report. Here is what your team will notice.
 

Veza Web

No runs on No-school days

You can now record the days a school is closed. Pick the type, set the first and last day, choose which schools it applies to, and add a note. Runs are not created for those days, so they do not reach the mobile app.

How to find it

Schools & Districts → No School → New No-School Day.

Good to know

A run is only skipped when every school it serves is closed that day. A run that also serves a school that is open still goes ahead.

Split a run into up to four runs

Splitting a run is no longer limited to two. You can now split into as many as four, and drag riders between the columns to decide who goes where. Split by school in the quick actions menu puts each school's riders into their own run in one step, for two to four schools. Tick Keep original if you want the original run to stay as well.

How to find it

Dispatch → open a run's details → Split run.

Late to School report

The late reasons and notes your dispatchers record on runs are now gathered into a report. It shows the reported school arrival time, the reported student pick-up or drop-off time, the minutes late, the reasons given and any notes. You can search the report and export it to CSV or Excel.

How to find it

Analytics → Late to School Report.

Required equipment on the Riders table

A new Required Equipment column on the Riders table shows the equipment each rider needs, and the column can be filtered, so you can list every rider who needs one piece of equipment at a time.

More detail when you pick a person for a role

In the lists where you choose someone for a role, each name now carries a second line with their unit number, the routes they are permanently assigned to, and their job title. This appears in both Dispatch and Routes.
