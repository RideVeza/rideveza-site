Week of October 5, 2026

Last week at Veza

Veza Web now has a charter system, named contacts for schools, districts and other clients, and updated holiday pay rules in Payroll. In the mobile app, drivers can now count riders who have no names at each stop. Here is what your team will notice.
 

Veza Web

⭐ Plan and manage charters

Charters now have their own page under Routes. Each charter gets its own charter ID and keeps the client and billing contact, the number of children, adults and units, required equipment, runs, riders and invoicing details in one place. Add team members with their roles and pay rates, and assign a unit to each driver. For the invoice, set a price for the whole charter or per unit, then add a discount and tax. When a run has a date and a stop with a time, Veza creates the matching run in Dispatch, with its team and pay. Track each charter through five statuses: Ready for review, Client approved, Runs completed, Invoice sent and Payment received. You can also export a PDF preview of the charter.

How to find it

Routes → Charters → Add charter. For a PDF, open a charter → the more menu → Export Preview.

Contacts for schools, districts and other clients

Other Clients now has its own page under Clients, next to Schools, Districts and No-School Days. Schools, districts and other clients can now have a list of named contacts, each with a name, email and phone number. Drag the contacts to put them in order, and choose one as the contact on a charter.

How to find it

Clients → Schools, Districts or Other Clients → open a client → Information tab → Contacts → Add

Updated holiday pay rules

Payroll now decides who gets holiday pay based on days worked. Active staff qualify when they have worked at least 30 different days in the year before the holiday. A day counts when they checked in and checked out of a run. Holiday pay uses the routes they are scheduled on for that weekday and the pay rates set on those routes.

How to find it

Payroll → Holiday → New Holiday to add a holiday and its date
 

Veza Mobile

Count unnamed riders at each stop

At each stop, the mobile app now shows a card for riders who have no names, with the number expected for pick-up or drop-off. Drivers enter how many actually got on or off with the minus and plus buttons, or by typing the number. A badge compares the count to the expected number, the card shows a warning when there are more riders than expected, and the rider counts on the map screen now include these riders.

How to find it

Open a run → go to a stop → the card marked Expected for pick-up or Expected for drop-off
