Week of October 12, 2026

Last week at Veza

Veza Web now shows street addresses for live bus positions, marks schools red when all riders are absent and adds new resolution statuses in Forms, and the mobile app adds new warnings for Start Run, the pre-trip check and unfinished earlier stops. Here is what your team will notice.
 

Veza Web

Street addresses for live bus positions

When you click a bus on the map, its popup shows when the bus was last seen. Click Show address in the popup to see the street address of that position. If the bus moves while the popup is open, the address updates.

How to find it

Dispatch or Units → click a bus on the map → Show address

School pill turns red when all riders are absent

On Dispatch, a school's pill now turns red when every rider for that school is marked absent. You can see these schools at a glance.

New resolution statuses, and resolutions on more form types

Form resolutions now have four statuses: Submitted, Assigned, In Progress and Resolved. Each status shows as a colored badge, next to the staff member it is assigned to and the resolution details. Pre-trip inspections, tire checks and route assessments now have a resolution too, like other form types.

How to find it

Forms → open a form → Status
 

Veza Mobile

Warnings when Start Run or the pre-trip check is not done

The mobile app now warns drivers who mark a rider before they tap Start Run, and they can start the run from the warning. If the pre-trip check for their unit is not done, a warning lets them switch to the unit they inspected today, pick another unit, or dismiss. Each warning shows once per run.

Warning when an earlier stop is not finished

The mobile app now warns drivers when they mark a rider while an earlier stop on the run still has riders who are not marked. The warning appears before the rider is marked.
