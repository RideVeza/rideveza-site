Week of September 7, 2026

Last week at Veza

In Veza Web, this week's updates improve how incident reports and guardian text messages get delivered, and add new details and options across Staff, Dispatch and Units. In the mobile app, passkey setup now gives drivers clearer guidance when something goes wrong. Here is what your team will notice.
 

Veza Web

Rider incident reports are emailed to the route supervisor

When a rider incident form is submitted, Veza now sends an email with the report attached as a PDF to the supervisor assigned on that run's route. The email goes to the supervisor's work email address in Staff, so the report reaches them without anyone forwarding it.

Improved deliverability of late and cover SMS to guardians

The text messages Veza sends to guardians when a run is late or a cover driver is assigned have been updated so more of them reach the phone. No setup is needed on your side.

Supervisor and Home address columns in the Staff table

The Staff table now shows the supervisor of each person's assigned routes and their home address. Both are regular columns, so you can sort and filter by them like any other column.

How to find it

Staff → look for the Supervisor and Home address columns in the table.

Cover driver is a late reason in Dispatch

When a run is late, Cover driver is now one of the reasons you can record on the run. This keeps late runs caused by a driver change separate from other reasons in your records.

How to find it

Dispatch → open a run's details → late reasons → choose Cover driver.

Improved Zonar GPS tracking in Dispatch

We reviewed and tuned the Zonar connection that feeds live bus positions into Dispatch. Bus locations on the Dispatch map refresh every 15 seconds while Dispatch is open, and Dispatch shows a GPS status when the provider is slow to respond.

How to find it

Dispatch → open a run's details → the map shows the unit's current position and today's GPS path.

Max Vest Anchor added to unit equipment

Max Vest Anchor is now an equipment option on units. You can record which buses have it and use it like any other equipment when matching units to routes.

How to find it

Fleet → open a unit's details → Equipment and Capabilities tab → Max Vest Anchor.
 

Veza Mobile

Clearer error messages when creating a passkey

When passkey setup or sign-in fails in the mobile app, the screen now shows a message that says what happened and what to do next. For example, if the phone has no screen lock or passcode, the message says to turn one on in the phone's settings; if the phone is too old for passkeys, it says which version to update to. This helps more drivers finish sign-up on their own and fix common problems without contacting your office.
