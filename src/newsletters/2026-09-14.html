Week of September 14, 2026

Last week at Veza

Veza Web adds an Excel import for routes and riders, and the mobile app adds a Unit Sign-in/out form whose submissions appear in Veza Web. Here is what your team will notice.
 

Veza Web

Import routes and riders from Excel

Routes can now be imported from an Excel workbook. Download the route import template, fill in routes and riders, choose the operational period and route dates, and Veza checks the workbook before anything is saved. The review lists new, modified and unchanged routes and riders and points to rows with issues, so you can correct them before importing.

How to find it

Routes → Add Route → Import from Excel → Download template, then upload the completed .xlsx file.
 

Veza Mobile

Unit Sign-in/out form

Drivers can sign a unit in or out from the mobile app. The form records the driver, unit, license plate, odometer and fuel level, a checklist, body defects and photos. Each submission appears in Veza Web with its details, checklist, defects and images, and drivers can see their own submissions under Submitted Forms.

How to find it

Forms → Unit Sign-in/out. In Veza Web: Units → Sign-In/Out to review submissions.
