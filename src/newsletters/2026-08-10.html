Week of August 10, 2026

Last week at Veza

Last week's changes are all in Veza Web. The biggest one changes what happens to your runs when you edit a route. Role assignments also picked up a new status, and route imports from BusPlanner bring across more detail. Here is what your team will notice.
 

Veza Web

Route edits only change the matching parts of a run

When you save a change to a route, Veza now updates only the part of each future run that the change applies to. The rest of the run stays as it is. You can edit a run in the future, and a later change to the route will not overwrite that edit unless it is a change to the same thing.

Good to know

Two things are still grouped together. Schedules go by shift: any change to the AM schedule rewrites the whole AM schedule on every AM run, so step times you changed on a single run are replaced. Assignees and pay rates are linked: change either one on the route, and both are updated on the runs it applies to.

New “Accepted” assignment status

A role assignment can now be set to Accepted: the person has accepted the role, but you are not ready for them to be on runs yet. Like Potential and Requested, an Accepted assignee is not put on runs and does not appear on the route PDF. Every assignment status now has its own colour and its own icon.

How to find it

Routes → open a route's details → set the status on a role assignment.

More detail comes across from BusPlanner

Route imports pick up more of the detail held in BusPlanner. A rider who uses a stop on only some days now comes in with the correct days on that stop. Car seats, harnesses and buckle guards come across onto the rider's care record. And where riders are picked up at a school, the bus is now scheduled to arrive ten minutes before the pickup time.

Job titles on the Assignments view

A new Assignee Job Title column shows the job title of the person in each role, and the column can be filtered, so you can see the assignments for one job title at a time.

How to find it

Routes → Assignments.
