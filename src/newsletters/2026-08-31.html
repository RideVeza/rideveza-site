Week of August 31, 2026

Last week at Veza

Congratulations on getting through the first week of school. Veza Web now shows a route's required equipment by shift and makes the role lists on Dispatch easier to search, work continued on how Zonar GPS data is handled, and two forms in the mobile app changed. Here is what your team will notice.
 

Veza Web

Required equipment by shift

A route's required equipment is now listed by shift. Each shift shows the equipment the riders on that shift need, with a count of how many riders need each item, so you can see what the bus must carry in the morning and in the afternoon. A shift with no equipment needs says so.

How to find it

Routes → open a route's details → Required Equipment.

Search role lists by unit, route or job title

When you choose a person for a role on Dispatch, the search now matches the second line under each name as well as the name itself. Type a unit number, a route name or a job title to narrow the list.

How to find it

Dispatch → open a run's details → choose a person for a role.

Better handling of Zonar GPS data

We changed how Veza works with the GPS data that comes from Zonar. Bus positions are now fetched once and shared across Dispatch instead of being requested separately by every open view, and ETAs are no longer refreshed more than once at the same time. If Zonar is responding slowly, the map in a run's details now shows a warning, and it goes away on its own once Zonar catches up. This work is still ongoing, and there is more to come over the next few weeks.

Dispatch filters open faster

The filter list on Dispatch loads faster, and the dropdown now grows to fit its options instead of scrolling inside a small box.
 

Veza Mobile

The unit is chosen from your fleet list on inspection forms

On the Pre-Trip Inspection and the Vehicle Defect Form, the unit must now be chosen from your fleet list. Typing a unit number that is not in the list is no longer an option. On the Pre-Trip Inspection, if no unit has been chosen, the Review step now lists Bus Information as incomplete, so the driver can see why Submit is not available.

How to find it

Forms → Pre-Trip Inspection or Vehicle Defect Form.

Time off requests cannot be set in the past

The time off request form no longer accepts a date or time that has already passed. On a one-day request, the end time must also be after the start time.
